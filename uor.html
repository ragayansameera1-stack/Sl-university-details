UNIVERSITY OF RUHUNA



HISTORY OF UNIVERSITY OF RUHUNA

  • 1978: Established as the Ruhuna University College by a Presidential decree.
  • 1984: Upgraded to a fully-fledged independent university as the University of Ruhuna.
  • 1984: Shifted its main campus complex to the current Wellamadama site in Matara.
  • Present: Expanded into a major higher education institute with campuses across Matara, Galle, and Hambantota.
