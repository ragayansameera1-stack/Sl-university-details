UNIVERSITY OF PERADENIYA

HISTORY OF UNIVERSITY OF PERADENIYA

  • 1942: The University of Ceylon was established in Colombo under the Ceylon University Ordinance. Plans were made to eventually relocate the main campus to Peradeniya.
  • 1944: Sir Patrick Abercrombie, a renowned British town planner, designed the master plan for the Peradeniya campus.
  • 1948: Construction of the Peradeniya campus began at the foot of the Hanthana Mountain Range, after clearing former tea and rubber plantations.
  • October 6, 1952: Academic activities officially began at Peradeniya when the Faculties of Arts and Agriculture were transferred from Colombo.
  • April 20, 1954: Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh, officially opened the Peradeniya campus. Sir Ivor Jennings was the first Vice-Chancellor of the University of Ceylon.
  • 1978: Under the Universities Act No. 16 of 1978, the institution became an independent university and was officially named the University of Peradeniya.
