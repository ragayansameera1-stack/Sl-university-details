HORIZON CAMPUS

(PREMIER PRIVATE HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTION IN SRI LANKA)



HISTORY OF HORIZON CAMPUS (PRIVATE CAMPUS)

  • 2011: Established as a private higher education institution in Malabe, Sri Lanka.
  • 2014: Granted official degree-awarding status by the Ministry of Higher Education.
  • 2019: Expanded strategic partnerships with top foreign universities in the UK and Malaysia.
  • Present: Operates as a prominent private campus offering diverse diploma, degree, and postgraduate programs.
