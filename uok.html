UNIVERSITY OF KELANIYA



HISTORY OF UNIVERSITY OF KELANIYA

  • 1875: Began as the Vidyalankara Pirivena, a renowned centre of Buddhist learning.
  • 1959: Elevated to university status under the Vidyodaya and Vidyalankara University Act.
  • 1978: Re-established as an autonomous institution named the University of Kelaniya.
