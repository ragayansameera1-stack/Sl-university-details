NSBM GREEN UNIVERSITY

(SRI LANKA'S PREMIER PRIVATE GREEN UNIVERSITY TOWN)



HISTORY OF NSBM (PRIVATE UNIVERSITY)

  • 2011: Established as the degree-awarding institute under the National Institute of Business Management (NIBM).
  • 2016: Declared open Sri Lanka's first fully-fledged Green University town complex in Pitipana, Homagama.
  • 2020: Expanded degree offerings with prestigious international partner universities from the UK and Australia.
  • Present: Recognized as a top self-financed non-state (Private) higher education institution in South Asia.
