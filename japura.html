UNIVERSITY OF JAYAWARDENEPURA

HISTORY OF UNIVERSITY OF JAYAWAEDENEPURA

  • 1873: Vidyodaya Pirivena was established at Maligakanda by Ven. Hikkaduwe Sri Sumangala Thero as an important centre of Oriental learning.
  • 1959: Under the Vidyodaya University Act No. 45 of 1958, it was elevated to university status and officially established as Vidyodaya University. Ven. Welivitiye Sri Soratha Thero served as its first Vice-Chancellor.
  • 1961: The university was relocated from Maligakanda to its present location in Gangodawila, Nugegoda.
  • 1966: The university was opened to female students, allowing women to pursue higher education there.
  • 1972: It was reorganized as the Vidyodaya Campus of the University of Sri Lanka.
  • 1978: Under the Universities Act No. 16 of 1978, it became an autonomous university and was officially renamed the University of Sri Jayewardenepura.
