INTERNATIONAL COLLEGE OF BUSINESS AND TECHNOLOGY (ICBT)

(SRI LANKA'S PREMIER PRIVATE HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTE FOR IT, ENGINEERING & MANAGEMENT)



HISTORY OF ICBT (PRIVATE INSTITUTE)

  • 1999: Founded as a leading private education provider in Sri Lanka to offer foreign qualifications locally.
  • 2000s: Partnered with renowned foreign universities such as Cardiff Metropolitan University (UK) to deliver internal degrees.
  • 2010s: Expanded network island-wide establishing modern branch campuses in Kandy, Southern, Jaffna, Kurunegala, and Batticaloa.
  • Present: Continues to be a pioneer private higher education institution offering Diploma, Bachelor's, and Master's programs.
