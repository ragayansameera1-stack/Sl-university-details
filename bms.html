BMS (BUSINESS MANAGEMENT SCHOOL)

(PREMIER PRIVATE HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTION IN SRI LANKA)



HISTORY OF BMS (PRIVATE INSTITUTE)

  • 1999: Established as a private higher education institution in Colombo, Sri Lanka.
  • 2002: Partnered with leading UK universities to offer British undergraduate and postgraduate qualifications.
  • 2010: Expanded facilities with state-of-the-art biotechnology and biomedical science laboratories.
  • Present: Renowned private institute producing high-caliber graduates in Management and Biomedical Sciences.
