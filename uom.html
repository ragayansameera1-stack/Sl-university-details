UNIVERSITY OF MORATUWA



HISTORY OF UNIVERSITY OF MORATUWA

  • 1893: Originated as the Government Technical School (GTS) established in Maradana.
  • 1966: Established as the Ceylon College of Technology (CCT) at Katubedda.
  • 1972: Became the Katubedda Campus of the University of Ceylon.
  • 1978: Gained independent university status as the University of Moratuwa.
