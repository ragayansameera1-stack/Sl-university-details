UNIVERSITY OF COLOMBO

HISTORY OF UNIVERSITY OF COLOMBO

1870: The Ceylon Medical School was established.
1921: The Ceylon University College was established to prepare students for degrees from the University of London.
1942: The Ceylon Medical College and Ceylon University College were incorporated into the newly established University of Ceylon, the country's first official university.
1967: The Colombo campus became an independent university known as the University of Ceylon, Colombo.
1972: It was renamed the University of Colombo.
