ROYAL INSTITUTE OF COLOMBO (RIC / RIO)

(SRI LANKA'S PREMIER PRIVATE HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTE FOR LAW, ENGINEERING & BUSINESS)



HISTORY OF ROYAL INSTITUTE (PRIVATE INSTITUTE)

  • 1971: Established as a pioneer private educational organization delivering primary to tertiary education.
  • 1991: Affiliated with the University of London to offer prestigious private international degree programs in Sri Lanka.
  • 2010s: Partnered with Deakin University, Australia, expanding private degree pathways in Engineering, Science, and IT.
  • Present: Stands as a leading private higher education provider with a strong reputation for academic excellence.
